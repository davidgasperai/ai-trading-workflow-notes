Title

Build Assets That Appreciate Over Time

Core Idea

Some work creates value only once.

Other work becomes more valuable every time it is reused.

Long-term projects benefit most from creating assets that continue to grow in usefulness rather than producing results with only short-lived impact.

Why It Matters

Daily news, individual analyses, and one-time conversations are valuable, but their usefulness often fades quickly.

In contrast, reusable knowledge, documented principles, evaluation frameworks, and well-designed workflows become stronger as the project grows. Each new contribution increases the value of everything that already exists.

Thinking in terms of appreciating assets encourages better long-term decisions.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Prefer reusable knowledge over temporary notes.
* Document principles that can guide future decisions.
* Build workflows that improve through repeated use.
* Treat Projects HQ as a growing strategic asset.
* Evaluate new work by its long-term value, not only its immediate usefulness.

Projects HQ Principle

Create knowledge that becomes more valuable every time it is used.
