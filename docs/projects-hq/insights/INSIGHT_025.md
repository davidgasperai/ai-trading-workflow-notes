Title

Review Milestones, Not Just Progress

Core Idea

Reaching a milestone is an opportunity to reflect, not just to celebrate.

Regular reviews help confirm what is working, identify unnecessary complexity, and ensure that growth remains aligned with the project’s long-term vision.

Why It Matters

Projects that never pause to review often accumulate technical debt and inconsistent ideas.

A structured review keeps the foundation strong while allowing continuous evolution.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Review the first 25 Insights as one connected body of knowledge.
* Identify recurring principles worth strengthening.
* Simplify areas that have become unnecessarily complex.
* Plan the next phase based on evidence, not momentum.

Projects HQ Principle

Milestones are checkpoints for learning, not finish lines.
