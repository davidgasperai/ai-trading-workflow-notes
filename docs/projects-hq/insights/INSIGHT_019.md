Title

Good Documentation Preserves Good Decisions

Core Idea

Great decisions lose much of their value if nobody remembers why they were made.

Documentation should capture not only outcomes, but also reasoning, assumptions and trade-offs.

Why It Matters

Projects often revisit the same questions because previous decisions were never properly recorded.

A documented decision saves future time and reduces repeated mistakes.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Record the reasoning behind important architectural choices.
* Document why ideas were accepted, rejected or postponed.
* Treat GitHub as the project’s institutional memory.
* Make future reviews faster and more objective.

Projects HQ Principle

A documented decision is knowledge that survives time.
