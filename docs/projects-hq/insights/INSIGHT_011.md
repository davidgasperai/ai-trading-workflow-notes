Title

Review Systems More Often Than You Redesign Them

Core Idea

Successful long-term projects improve through regular review rather than frequent reinvention.

Small adjustments based on real experience usually create better outcomes than repeatedly replacing existing systems.

Why It Matters

When a workflow is replaced too quickly, the project loses the opportunity to learn from accumulated experience.

Consistent review helps identify genuine weaknesses while preserving what already works well. Stable systems become stronger because improvements are evolutionary instead of disruptive.

Continuous review also builds confidence in the architecture and prevents unnecessary complexity.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Review workflows before redesigning them.
* Base improvements on evidence collected over time.
* Preserve successful processes unless there is a clear reason to change them.
* Document lessons learned after each major milestone.
* Let experience drive evolution.

Projects HQ Principle

Improve through observation before rebuilding through innovation.
