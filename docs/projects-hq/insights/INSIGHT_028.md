Title

Trust Should Be Designed, Not Assumed

Core Idea

Reliable systems do not depend on perfect behavior.

They earn trust by making errors observable, limiting their impact, and allowing independent verification.

Why It Matters

As AI agents become more capable, assuming they will always behave correctly becomes increasingly risky.

Trust emerges from architecture, transparency, and controlled permissions—not from optimism.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Design every agent with explicit permission boundaries.
* Make important actions traceable through logs.
* Prefer verification over assumptions.
* Build review points before irreversible operations.

Projects HQ Principle

Trust grows from verifiable design, not from good intentions.
