Date: 13. 08. 2026

Title

Adaptation Must Earn Its Way Into Production

Core Idea

A system should be allowed to learn from failure without being allowed to rewrite its own standards of success.

Adaptation is valuable.

Uncontrolled adaptation is optimization without accountability.

Every meaningful change should therefore pass through the same validation process that justified the original system.

Why It Matters

AI agents can increasingly analyze their own failures and propose improvements.

This creates enormous potential—but also a dangerous shortcut.

After a losing trade or disappointing experiment, an agent may identify a plausible explanation and modify its own rules.

If those modifications immediately become part of the production system, learning quietly becomes overfitting.

The system is no longer testing hypotheses.

It is rewriting history until the past looks explainable.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Agents may propose strategy modifications.
* Production strategies should never self-modify directly.
* Every proposed change receives a unique version.
* The original hypothesis and failure evidence remain preserved.
* Modified rules return to research status.
* Validation starts again using untouched data.
* Walk-forward and OOS requirements remain unchanged.
* Transaction costs and execution assumptions remain mandatory.
* Failed validation means rejection—not another automatic rewrite.
* Only validated versions may progress toward paper trading or production.

Projects HQ Principle

Learning may propose a change. Evidence must authorize it.

Future Applications

* Walk-forward validation pipeline
* Production promotion criteria
* Rollback to last validated strategy
