Title

Review Before You Expand

Core Idea

Growth is valuable only if the foundation remains stable.

Before adding new features, revisit existing systems, confirm that they still support your goals, and simplify wherever possible.

Why It Matters

Many projects become unnecessarily complex because they keep expanding without periodic review.

Regular reflection prevents technical debt, duplicated work and drifting priorities.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Review existing workflows before introducing new ones.
* Prefer refining proven systems over constantly creating new ones.
* Keep documentation synchronized with reality.
* Schedule regular architecture reviews as the project grows.

Projects HQ Principle

Strong foundations allow sustainable growth.
