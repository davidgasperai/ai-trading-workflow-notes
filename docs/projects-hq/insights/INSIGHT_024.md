Date: 1. 8. 2026

Title

Architecture Should Evolve, Principles Should Endure

Core Idea

Technology changes quickly.

Strong principles should remain stable, while architecture adapts to new capabilities.

The best long-term projects continuously improve their implementation without abandoning their core philosophy.

Why It Matters

Projects that tie themselves to one model or one tool eventually become obsolete.

Projects built on enduring principles can adopt new technologies with minimal disruption.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Keep the philosophy stable.
* Update the architecture when better approaches emerge.
* Replace tools without replacing the system.
* Let knowledge outlive individual AI models.

Projects HQ Principle

Adapt the architecture. Preserve the principles.
