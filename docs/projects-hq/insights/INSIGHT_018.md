Title

Build Systems That Can Learn

Core Idea

A successful system does more than execute tasks.

It continuously learns from new information, documents decisions, and improves without losing its core principles.

Learning should be built into the process—not left to chance.

Why It Matters

Markets change.

AI changes.

Technology changes.

A project that can absorb new knowledge while preserving its long-term philosophy will adapt more effectively than one built around fixed assumptions.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Record important decisions, not just outcomes.
* Review workflows regularly and refine them using new evidence.
* Preserve core principles while allowing implementations to evolve.
* Treat every completed experiment as new knowledge for the system.

Projects HQ Principle

The best systems don’t just work—they learn.
