# 🐸 PROJECTS HQ INSIGHT #004

**Date:** 11. 07. 2026

## Title

**Architecture Outlives Models**

## Core Idea

Individual AI models will continue to evolve rapidly, but a well-designed system architecture can remain valuable across multiple generations of models.

## Why It Matters

Today's leading model may be replaced within months. Chasing every new release creates unnecessary complexity and "model FOMO."

For **Project Nekonečný Mír**, long-term value comes from building a stable architecture based on clearly separated responsibilities, reproducible evaluation, documented decisions, and well-defined workflows.

A robust architecture makes replacing individual models straightforward without redesigning the entire system.

## Impact on Nekonečný Mír

- Design the project around architecture rather than specific AI models.
- Separate responsibilities between specialized agents.
- Keep evaluation procedures independent of any individual model.
- Document architectural decisions and reasoning in GitHub Projects HQ.
- Treat AI models as interchangeable components within a stable system.

## Projects HQ Principle

> **Strong architecture survives multiple generations of AI models.**
