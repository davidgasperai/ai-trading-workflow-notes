Title

Every Experiment Spends Statistical Trust

Core Idea

Testing more ideas does not automatically create more knowledge.

Every additional strategy, parameter set, filter, indicator, or hypothesis tested against the same historical data increases the probability of discovering an apparently successful result by chance.

Research capacity can scale rapidly.

Statistical confidence cannot be treated as unlimited.

Why It Matters

AI can generate and test hypotheses at a scale that would be impossible for a human researcher.

This creates an enormous opportunity for Nekonečný Mír.

It also creates a new form of risk.

If thousands of strategy variants are tested and only the best result is retained, the winning backtest may reflect repeated exposure to historical data rather than genuine predictive structure.

The danger is therefore not only traditional parameter overfitting.

The research process itself can overfit.

A trustworthy system must remember how many opportunities it had to find something that looked successful.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Record every meaningful strategy experiment, including failures.
* Track how many hypotheses and variants were tested.
* Never evaluate the final winner as if it were the only experiment performed.
* Separate hypothesis generation from final validation data.
* Protect untouched OOS data from repeated research exposure.
* Use stricter evidence requirements as research throughput increases.
* Preserve rejected experiments instead of silently deleting them.
* Avoid repeatedly modifying a strategy until historical data approves it.
* Treat exceptionally strong backtests with greater skepticism when they emerge from large searches.
* Promote strategies based on robustness, not leaderboard position.

Projects HQ Principle

The more chances a system has to find a winner, the more evidence that winner must provide.
