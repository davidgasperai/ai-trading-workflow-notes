Title

Safety Must Scale with Capability

Core Idea

As AI systems become more capable, safety cannot remain static.

Every increase in capability should be matched by stronger testing, monitoring, and safeguards. Long-term success comes not only from building more powerful systems, but from building systems that remain trustworthy as they evolve.

Why It Matters

Today’s AI can already perform complex multi-step tasks. As autonomous agents gain more responsibility, small design weaknesses can have much larger consequences.

A resilient project treats safety as part of the architecture—not as a feature added at the end.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Design agent workflows with clear boundaries and review points.
* Keep humans responsible for important decisions.
* Validate new capabilities before integrating them into production.
* Document safety principles alongside technical architecture.
* Consider reliability and transparency as long-term competitive advantages.

Projects HQ Principle

Greater capability requires greater responsibility.
