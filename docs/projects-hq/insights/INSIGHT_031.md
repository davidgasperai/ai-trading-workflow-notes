Title

Permission Is Not Permanent Trust

Core Idea

Authorization should reflect the current state of the system, not only the conditions that existed when permission was originally granted.

An action that was safe when proposed may no longer be safe when executed.

Why It Matters

AI agents can operate across long workflows where market conditions, account balances, risk limits, data, approvals, or system state may change between decision and execution.

Static permission alone is therefore insufficient.

Critical actions should be validated against the latest available state immediately before they become irreversible.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Treat permissions as conditional rather than permanent.
* Revalidate critical actions immediately before execution.
* Check current risk limits, market state, and account conditions.
* Expire approvals when relevant assumptions materially change.
* Never allow an old authorization to bypass new information.
* Keep live trading behind a final runtime validation gate.

Projects HQ Principle

Permission grants authority. Revalidation confirms that the authority is still safe to use.
