Title

A Benchmark Is Evidence Only for the Environment It Measured

Core Idea

Performance does not exist independently of the environment in which it was measured.

A strategy can rank first in one period and fail in another without either result being fraudulent.

The mistake begins when a local result is treated as evidence of universal capability.

Why It Matters

Short trading evaluations are particularly vulnerable to:

* market regime,
* volatility,
* asset selection,
* sample size,
* event timing,
* transaction costs,
* and simple luck.

AI increases this problem because many agents, models and configurations can be compared simultaneously.

A leaderboard can therefore identify the best performer inside one experiment without establishing which system possesses durable edge.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Never promote a strategy because it wins a single leaderboard.
* Record the environment in which every result was produced.
* Separate comparative performance from evidence of absolute edge.
* Require performance across multiple independent windows where appropriate.
* Track regime sensitivity explicitly.
* Preserve rankings from failed as well as successful periods.
* Avoid changing evaluation windows after observing results.
* Do not infer general trading intelligence from one asset.
* Include costs and execution assumptions in comparisons.
* Treat unusually strong short-window performance as a hypothesis requiring more evidence.

Projects HQ Principle

A result describes what happened under tested conditions. It does not automatically describe what will happen elsewhere.
