Title

Speed Must Never Bypass Evidence

Core Idea

Faster intelligence can accelerate good decisions.

It can also accelerate bad ones.

As AI systems become faster, the validation requirements governing their actions should remain independent of inference speed.

Why It Matters

A slow agent may analyze one hypothesis in ten minutes.

A future agent may analyze hundreds in the same time.

That sounds like pure progress.

But faster hypothesis generation also creates faster multiple-testing, faster overfitting, faster policy drift, and faster execution of mistakes.

The bottleneck in a trustworthy system therefore should not be how quickly intelligence can generate an idea.

The bottleneck should be how rigorously evidence must validate it before consequences follow.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Faster models may increase research throughput.
* Validation standards must not loosen as throughput rises.
* Every strategy candidate remains subject to the same OOS rules.
* More experiments require stronger multiple-testing discipline.
* Production promotion cannot be accelerated merely because inference is faster.
* Risk gates remain independent of model latency.
* Live execution remains downstream of validation and authorization.
* A faster agent must never receive automatic permission to take larger risks.

Projects HQ Principle

Intelligence may accelerate. Evidence keeps its own clock.
