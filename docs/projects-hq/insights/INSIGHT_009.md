Title

Simple Systems Are Easier to Improve

Core Idea

Complexity often grows gradually, one small addition at a time.

Simple systems are easier to understand, maintain, test, and improve because every component has a clear purpose.

Long-term scalability comes from disciplined simplicity rather than accumulating features.

Why It Matters

Projects rarely become difficult because of one major mistake. More often, they become harder to manage through many small, unnecessary additions.

A simple architecture makes it easier to identify problems, replace components, and adapt to new technologies without disrupting the entire system.

Simplicity is not the absence of capability—it is the result of thoughtful design.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Prefer clear workflows over complicated ones.
* Add new components only when they solve a real problem.
* Review existing processes before creating new ones.
* Keep documentation focused and easy to navigate.
* Let architecture evolve deliberately, not accidentally.

Projects HQ Principle

Every unnecessary layer today becomes tomorrow’s maintenance burden.
