Title

Systems Should Welcome Change

Core Idea

The strongest systems are not those that resist change, but those that are designed to absorb it with minimal disruption.

Why It Matters

AI models, tools and APIs evolve rapidly. If a project depends on a single implementation, every major update becomes a risk. A modular architecture allows improvement without rebuilding the entire system.

Impact on Nekonečný Mír

* Design clear interfaces between components.
* Keep AI models replaceable.
* Separate principles from implementation details.
* Make documentation the stable reference point.

Projects HQ Principle

A resilient system embraces change without losing its identity.
