# 🐸 PROJECTS HQ INSIGHT #005

**Date:** 12. 07. 2026

## Title

**Knowledge Outlasts News**

## Core Idea

Daily news has a short lifespan.

Well-formulated principles can remain valuable for years.

Instead of collecting hundreds of articles, Project **Nekonečný Mír** should capture the underlying lessons that continue to guide decisions long after today's headlines are forgotten.

## Why It Matters

Most news becomes irrelevant within days.

The principles extracted from those events, however, can improve future architecture, research workflows, risk management, and strategic decision-making.

A growing collection of validated insights becomes a long-term competitive advantage and gradually transforms Projects HQ into the strategic memory of the project.

## Impact on Nekonečný Mír

- Capture timeless principles instead of temporary headlines.
- Preserve documented reasoning, not only conclusions.
- Build reusable knowledge rather than storing isolated information.
- Review every daily briefing through the lens of long-term value.
- Continuously strengthen Projects HQ as the project's strategic knowledge base.

## Projects HQ Principle

> **A news archive stores information. A knowledge base stores understanding.**
