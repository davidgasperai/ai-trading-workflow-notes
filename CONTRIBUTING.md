# Contributing

Contributions and ideas are welcome.

## Guidelines

- Prefer small and meaningful commits.
- Document important decisions.
- Keep research notes organized.
- Reusable templates are preferred over ad-hoc notes.
- Transparency and reproducibility are important principles.

## Repository Philosophy

The goal of this repository is learning, documentation, and experimentation around AI-assisted workflows.

This repository does not provide financial advice or trading signals.
