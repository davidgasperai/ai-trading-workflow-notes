# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to creating an open, welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences
- Focusing on learning and collaboration
- Providing helpful feedback
- Supporting transparent and reproducible research practices

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Trolling or insulting comments
- Misleading or intentionally false information

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards when necessary.
