# Research Principles

## Core Mindset

- Start with a clear question.
- Separate observations from conclusions.
- Avoid forcing a result.
- Prefer evidence over excitement.
- Document uncertainty clearly.

## AI-Assisted Research

- Use AI to organize thinking, not to replace judgment.
- Review important conclusions manually.
- Keep prompts and outputs understandable.
- Avoid blindly trusting generated answers.
- Treat AI suggestions as hypotheses.

## Trading Research

- Do not treat backtests as proof.
- Validate ideas across different periods.
- Watch for overfitting.
- Compare results against simple baselines.
- Record platform or data issues.

## Review Process

- Summarize what was tested.
- Record what changed.
- State what remains unknown.
- Decide the next step before continuing.
